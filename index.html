Before you start

This test checks whether you are a native Afrikaans speaker. It takes about 10 minutes.

Who can take this test:
  • Native Afrikaans speakers living in South Africa
  • A South African WhatsApp number (+27) is required
  • 18 years or older
Every person must take this test individually with their own phone number and their own voice. Team leaders cannot submit on behalf of their members.
The test has 3 parts:
  1. Read a short Afrikaans text aloud (voice recording)
  2. 12 multiple-choice questions (6-minute time limit)
  3. Short writing and correction tasks
Rules: Do not use Google Translate, ChatGPT or any other help. Do not leave this page during the quiz and writing parts. Leaving the page and pasting text are recorded automatically. Each number can submit only once.
